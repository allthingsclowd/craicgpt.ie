I've met Shane on and off these past fifteen years, but I know him mainly by the songs he sings. More than any writer I've heard, his lyrics bring me to a place that I know. I know his country, I've been through his meadow, across his bog, down his street and I love his work dearly. I sang with him once a version of 'Spancilhill. I had recorded the song in 1971 and when we rehearsed it in 1995 he pulled me up for having changed it he is a man of words and raw emotion and understated pain that comes crawling out of the lyrics like the madness crawling out of the mountain. When he sent me 'Aisling' I could not decipher a melody so I made one up and I hope he likes it.
